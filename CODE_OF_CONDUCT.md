# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.

## Our pledge

We want this to be a welcoming place for everyone who builds for the web, whatever their experience, background, identity or language. English isn't everyone's first language here; clarity matters more than polish.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good intent.
- Critique work, never people. Design feedback is welcome when it is specific and useful.
- Credit others' work and ideas.
- Respect privacy: no client names, private URLs or personal details in issues, PRs or examples unless their owner has agreed.

## What we don't accept

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Sexualized language or imagery.
- Publishing others' private information without their permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Enforcement

Report problems to the maintainer through the contact listed on [@vvhybe's GitHub profile](https://github.com/vvhybe). Reports are handled privately. The maintainer may remove comments, commits or contributions, and may ban contributors, temporarily or permanently, for behavior that breaks this code.

This code applies in all project spaces and when someone represents the project in public.
